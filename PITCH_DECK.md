## Slide 10 — Future Roadmap

**Automatic brokerage sync via official APIs (next step)**

- **Plaid** — bank and brokerage account linking with user-permissioned, read-only aggregation (industry-standard pattern for wealth dashboards).
- **Schwab API** — where available, institution-official programmatic access for balances and positions (subject to Schwab developer and compliance programs).
- **Fidelity API** — same framing: official channels only, no screen-scraping; aligns with how enterprise wealth platforms integrate.

This shows judges we know where the product goes **without** claiming live Plaid/Schwab/Fidelity integrations are in the hackathon build. The current demo uses **manual account entry** and **manual holding logs** for analysis and instructions only—not trade execution.
