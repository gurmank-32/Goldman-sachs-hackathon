Problem Statement
Design and build a comprehensive, user-friendly Stock and Mutual Fund portfolio management system tailored specifically for non-market-savy individuals.
The platform must demystify wealth management, allowing users to easily track their investments, understand their risk exposure, and seamlessly rebalance their portfolios in response to changing personal needs or uncertain macroeconomic scenarios.
